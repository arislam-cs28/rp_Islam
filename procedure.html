Procedure

A deeper dive into the materials and methodology.


Step

Description

Image

1

Prepare each of the Drosophila vials

  • Leave culture of 50 pupae to rest until eclosion
  • Mix 3.8 g of Formula 4-24 Instant Drosophila Blue Medium with 8 mL distilled water
  • Deposit an even number of flies into each vial until you have 8 vials

2

Prepare plastic treatments for each experimental group

  • Blend 27.4 g of food-grade PVC sheet with 500 mL of water
  • Blend 27.4 g of food-grade PVC sheet with 500 mL of water
  • Dry and filter both microplastic blends with a 0.75 mm diameter hole sieve

3

Prepare experimental group vials

  • In three of six vials, place in the powder of a single testosterone capsule for PCOS
  • In two vials, one PCOS and one non-PCOS, place no plastic
  • In two vials, one PCOS and one non-PCOS, place 1.5 g of PET
  • In two vials, one PCOS and one non-PCOS, place 1.5 g of PVC
  • Place 30 flies into each experimental vial

4

Dissect experimental flies

  • Place a 6 in ruler on a stereomicroscope for scale
  • Aneshtetize all flies, and place one on phosphate-buffered saline on the microscope stage
  • Tug at the end of the Drosophila with fine-tip forceps until ovaries come out
  • Photograph ovaries and repeat for all

5

Measure ovary sizes with ImageJ software

  • Place each image into the software
  • Get the scale from the scale reference
  • Measure the length, width, and upper surface area of each ovary

6

Conduct MANOVA data analyses with Python in VSCode

  • Using a Jupyter notevook, place all data into CSV files
  • Import numpy, pandas, seaborn, and matplotlib.pyplot to take statistics
  • Conduct MANOVA calculations, collect Pillai's trace, and graph with seaborn and matplotlib
